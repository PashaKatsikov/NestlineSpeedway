Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 2026

Nestline Speedway does not collect any personal data. The game runs entirely offline on your device. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party trackers.

Information We Collect

None. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any information about you or your device. The app contains no advertising identifiers, no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, and no login of any kind.

Data Stored On Your Device

So that you can continue a career between sessions, the game writes its own save file to your device's private application storage. That save contains only game content:

  • Your birds, their genomes and pedigrees
  • Season progress, grain, eggs, tack and consumables
  • Codex discoveries and stable upgrades
  • Sound and music preferences

This data never leaves your device. It is not readable by us or by other apps, and it is removed by the operating system when you uninstall the app. You can also erase it at any time with Found a new stable in the game's Settings screen.

Network Use

The game does not require an internet connection to play, and it does not contact any server of ours. The only screens that display web content are this policy and the support page, both of which are bundled inside the app and loaded from local files.

Children's Privacy

Because the app collects no data at all, it collects no data from children. Nestline Speedway contains no chat, no user-generated content, no social features, no advertising, and no purchases of any kind.

Your Rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and CCPA grant you rights to access, correct, export, or delete personal data held about you. We hold no such data, so there is nothing for us to retrieve or erase. You remain in full control of the on-device save file as described above.

Changes To This Policy

If a future version of the app ever changes what it stores or sends, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the effective date above will change.
