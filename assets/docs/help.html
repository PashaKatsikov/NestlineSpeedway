Nestline Speedway Support

Nestline Speedway is a turn-based racing game where the commands you can play in a race are decided by the genes of the bird you bred. Below are the questions we are asked most often.

How do I get better commands?

Commands come from alleles. Breed two birds that each carry the allele you want and a quarter of the hatchlings will express it. The Hatchery forecast shows the odds before you spend an egg. Pairing two expressed traits from the same locus can also unlock a synergy command that neither parent had.

Why did my bird stop moving mid-race?

Stamina hit zero, so the bird is blown: effort per turn drops and most move commands are unaffordable. Guard or a form command lets stamina recover. Watch the terrain strip at the top of the screen — mud and hills charge extra stamina, so bank some before you reach them.

My best line keeps producing frail chicks.

That is inbreeding. Every bird shows an F value; past 0.125 you start taking hatch and stamina penalties. Buy unrelated stock from a trader and cross it back in to reset the coefficient.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically, on your device only. There are no accounts and no cloud sync, so uninstalling the app or tapping Found a new stable in Settings erases a career permanently.

Does the game need the internet or cost money?

No. It plays fully offline and contains no ads, no purchases, and no subscriptions.

Still stuck, or found a bug? Tell us what happened, which screen you were on, and what device you are using, and we will get back to you.
